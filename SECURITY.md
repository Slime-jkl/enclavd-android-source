Scope: We welcome security reports regarding the Enclavd web platform and its associated mobile applications.

## Guidelines:
- Do No Harm: Do not access, modify, or destroy user data.
- Respect Privacy: If you encounter private user data, stop immediately and report it.
- Coordination: Please report vulnerabilities via security@enclavd.com and allow us reasonable time to respond before disclosing information publicly.

## What we reward:

We currently operate a recognition-based program. 
We will acknowledge your contribution on our Security Hall of Fame page if your report is valid and unique.

Hall of Fame - https://enclavd.com/hall-of-fame
